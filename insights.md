# Serenity Threads Sdn. Bhd. (fictional) — FY2025 Key Findings

**CONFIDENTIAL — internal use only**

1. **FY2025 result: Net Loss of RM871,733.** Sales were RM905,108 but purchases and direct costs (COGS) totaled RM1,274,914 — a gross loss of RM369,806 before any operating expenses. Operating expenses (rent, salaries, etc.) of RM501,927 added to that, bringing the net loss to the figure above. This is the single most important number in the accounts and is worth discussing directly with whoever manages the business finances.

2. **Salaries, wages & allowances (RM307,824) is the largest operating expense at 34% of total sales**, followed by shop rental (RM84,156). Together these two fixed costs alone consume a large share of revenue regardless of how sales perform month to month.

3. **Textile Mill A accounts for 73% of all supplier purchases** (RM884,424 of RM1,212,635 total). This is a very high concentration in a single supplier — a pricing, quality, or supply disruption from this one vendor would have an outsized effect on the whole business.

4. **Wholesale Client A is the largest customer at 48% of ledger sales** (RM370,678). Note this ledger total (RM769,018) is lower than the P&L sales figure (RM905,108) — likely because some sales (e.g. daily TikTok/Shopee Live or till transactions) are batched elsewhere rather than posted per named customer. Worth confirming with the bookkeeper before treating the customer-level split as complete.

5. **Outstanding receivables (RM361,410) vs. payables (RM394,422)** at period end. On the payable side, RM350,278 alone is owed to Textile Mill A. On the receivable side, Wholesale Client A owes RM361,410. Separately, Wholesale Client B is showing a RM185,822 *credit* balance as a customer (paid more than they've been invoiced for) — a deposit/advance held, not money owed to the business. A small number of large one-off relationships dominate both sides of the ledger rather than broad-based amounts across many accounts.
